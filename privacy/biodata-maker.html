Biodata Maker — Privacy Policy

App: Biodata Maker: Tamil Jathagam (com.teksxt.lifestyle.biodatamaker) · Publisher: Teksxt · Effective 10 October 2026

Summary

Biodata Maker has no account and no signup. Everything you type into a biodata — names, dates, horoscope details, phone numbers, addresses — and every photo you add is stored only on your phone. We never receive it. It leaves your phone only when you choose to share, print or back it up.

Information stored on your phone

Biodata details, photos (cropped copies of the images you choose), jathagam photos and your settings are kept in the app's private storage. They are excluded from Android cloud backup. Uninstalling the app or tapping Delete removes them. "Back up to file" writes a file to a location you pick; we never see that file.

Permissions

Internet (for purchases, the optional rewarded ad and anonymous analytics) and the advertising ID (for AdMob). The camera opens through Android's own camera app only when you tap Camera; photos are chosen through Android's photo picker, which gives the app access only to the photos you select.

Children

The app is intended for adults preparing marriage biodatas and is not directed at children under 13.

Your choices

You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in Android settings. You can delete any biodata in the app, or all app data by uninstalling. Analytics collection is limited to the events described above.
